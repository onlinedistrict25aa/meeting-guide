About Our District Logo

Connected Through Service

The interconnected figures represent A.A. groups and trusted servants united through service. Their circular arrangement reflects the connection between groups, the district, and the area, while the central globe symbolizes the ability of online A.A. service to extend beyond geographical boundaries.

Together, these elements express unity, participation, and our shared responsibility to carry the message.
